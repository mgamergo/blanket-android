# Blanket

Blanket combines ambient sounds into a personal listening environment.

## Language

**Sound**:
A built-in or custom ambient recording that can repeat independently.

**Mix**:
The enabled sounds and their individual volume levels.

**Preset**:
A named, automatically saved mix. Default is the permanent initial preset.

**Main volume**:
The global loudness multiplier applied to the whole mix.

**Paused**:
Playback is suspended while the selected sounds and their volume levels are retained.
